# Microsoft Service Trust Portal and Privacy Principles

## Service Trust Portal

The Microsoft Service Trust Portal provides information about Microsoft’s security, privacy and compliance practices.

It includes resources such as:

- Audit reports
- Compliance reports and certifications
- Security and privacy documentation
- Data protection information
- Compliance guides and trust documents

Some resources require users to sign in with a Microsoft account.

---

## Microsoft Privacy Principles

Microsoft follows six main privacy principles:

- **Control** – Customers control their personal data.
- **Transparency** – Microsoft explains how data is collected and used.
- **Security** – Data is protected through strong security measures.
- **Strong legal protections** – Microsoft respects privacy laws and protects customer data from inappropriate government access.
- **No content-based targeting** – Customer data is not used for targeted advertising.
- **Benefits to customers** – Data is used to improve customer experiences and services.

---

## Key Takeaways

- The Service Trust Portal provides Microsoft security, privacy and compliance documentation.
- Organizations can use its reports to support audits and compliance assessments.
- Microsoft’s privacy principles focus on customer control, transparency and security.
- Microsoft protects customer data through legal, technical and organizational measures.
