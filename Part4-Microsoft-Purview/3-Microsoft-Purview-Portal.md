# Microsoft Purview Data Compliance Solutions

## Microsoft Purview Portal

The Microsoft Purview portal provides a centralized location for managing data security, governance, risk, and compliance solutions.

---

## Compliance Manager

Compliance Manager helps organizations assess and manage compliance across multicloud environments.

It provides:

- Compliance assessments
- Recommended improvement actions
- A compliance score
- Progress monitoring

---

## Audit

Microsoft Purview Audit records user and administrator activities.

### Audit (Standard)

Provides tools for searching and reviewing audit events.

### Audit (Premium)

Provides advanced auditing capabilities for forensic investigations, security incidents, and regulatory requirements.

---

## eDiscovery

eDiscovery helps organizations identify, preserve, collect, review, and export electronic information that may be used as evidence in legal investigations.

---

## Communication Compliance

Communication Compliance detects potentially inappropriate or risky communications.

It can identify:

- Harassment
- Threatening language
- Regulatory violations
- Sharing of sensitive information

It is part of Microsoft Purview's broader insider risk capabilities.

---

## Data Lifecycle Management

Data Lifecycle Management controls how long organizational data is retained and when it is deleted.

It uses:

- Retention policies
- Retention labels

---

## Records Management

Records Management manages regulatory, legal, and business-critical records throughout their lifecycle.

It helps ensure that important records are retained, protected, and disposed of correctly.

---

## Key Takeaways

- The Microsoft Purview portal centralizes compliance management.
- Compliance Manager measures and improves compliance posture.
- Audit records and investigates user and administrator activities.
- eDiscovery supports legal and regulatory investigations.
- Communication Compliance identifies risky communications.
- Data Lifecycle Management controls data retention and deletion.
- Records Management protects important organizational records.
